# Umoja Afya 10.8 — Multicultural synthetic data and landing refresh

## Synthetic patient population

The review seed now targets 15,000 synthetic patients. Names are drawn from culturally appropriate Tanzanian, Kenyan, Nigerian, Ugandan and South African name sets. Uganda and South Africa are represented as regional cross-border residents or visitors within the enabled Tanzania, Kenya and Nigeria practice contexts so country-access isolation remains enforceable.

All records are synthetic and marked as review data. Identifier, telephone, payer and address conventions vary by practice context and cultural profile.

## Landing page

The country landing page was condensed to reduce unused space and improve scanability. It now uses a compact hero, horizontal country cards, clear access steps, and a concise authenticated-context notice. Facility and unit lists remain behind login.
