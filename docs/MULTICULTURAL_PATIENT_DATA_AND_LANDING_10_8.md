# Umoja Afya 10.8 — Multicultural synthetic data and landing refresh

## Synthetic patient population

The review seed targets 15,000 synthetic patients. Names are drawn from culturally appropriate Tanzanian, Kenyan, Nigerian, Pakistani, Rwandan, Ugandan and South African name sets. Uganda and South Africa are represented as regional cross-border residents or visitors within compatible enabled practice contexts so country-access isolation remains enforceable.

All records are synthetic and marked as review data. Identifier, telephone, payer and address conventions vary by practice context and cultural profile.

## Landing page

The 10.16.2 country landing page uses a compact five-country selector, responsive care-context hero, clearer access steps and a concise authenticated-context notice. Facility and unit lists remain behind login.
