# Production Roadmap

## Controlled pilot completion

- Approve national and facility workflow specifications.
- Complete current-state system, interface, device and data inventory.
- Validate identity matching and migration rules.
- Configure government SSO, MFA, access lifecycle and emergency access.
- Complete DPIA, threat model, clinical-safety case and hazard log.
- Validate clinical terminology, order sets, medication content and advisories.
- Complete load, failover, backup, restore, downtime and reconciliation testing.
- Conduct user acceptance and simulation with registration, clinical, ancillary, finance, ICT and management teams.

## First-wave integrations

- NIDA identity verification where legally approved
- NHIF/UHI eligibility, authorization and claims
- GePG payment and reconciliation
- DHIS2 and eIDSR reporting
- eLMIS/MSD inventory exchange
- HFR, HRHIS and professional registries
- Laboratory analyzers, PACS/DICOM and selected medical devices

## Scale requirements

- National product and clinical-content governance
- Regional support and command centers
- Controlled configuration and release management
- Data-quality monitoring and benefits realization
- Training certification and permanent clinical-informatics capacity

## Deferred product stream

The consumer portal, patient mobile application and direct patient messaging experience will be designed as a separate future stream after provider-EHR workflows, identity, consent and national integration foundations are accepted.
