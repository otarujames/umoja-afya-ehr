# Alignment with the Ministry Proposal

The application structure follows the proposal’s central design decisions:

- One national longitudinal patient identity and record.
- Modular activation by facility and service level.
- Integration-first treatment of existing government digital-health assets.
- Government control of hosting, data, encryption keys, interfaces and access policy.
- Standards-based exchange using FHIR, HL7 and DICOM.
- Closed-loop registration, patient flow, orders/results, medication, referral, discharge and surveillance workflows.
- Kiswahili/English, low-bandwidth and patient-access requirements.
- Clinical decision-support governance, audit, privacy and independent clinical-safety assurance.
- MNH first-wave deployment with connected specialist institutions and later national scale.

The current release implements the operational core required to demonstrate the concept. It is not a substitute for the proposed national workflow workgroup, implementation-readiness inventory, clinical content governance, DPIA, conformance testing or independent assurance.
