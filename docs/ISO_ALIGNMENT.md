# ISO Management-System Alignment

## ISO/IEC 27001:2022

The package includes controls and artefacts supporting an Information Security Management System: asset and access management, secure operations, supplier controls, incident management, continuity, risk treatment and evidence tracking.

## ISO/IEC 27701:2025

The package includes Privacy Information Management System support: controller/processor accountability, purpose and minimization, consent/proxy context, retention, disclosure controls, privacy risk assessment and data-subject workflows.

## ISO 27799:2025

Health-specific security support includes clinical confidentiality, patient identity, emergency access, health-record integrity, auditability, workflow availability and controlled clinical content.

## ISO 22301:2019

Business-continuity artefacts cover impact analysis, recovery objectives, backup/restore, downtime operations, exercise records and continuous improvement.

Certification requires implementation by the organization and audit by an appropriately accredited certification body.
