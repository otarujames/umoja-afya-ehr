# Clinical Safety Case Template

## Scope

Identify the release, facilities, modules, users, interfaces, clinical pathways and excluded capabilities.

## Safety responsibilities

Name the Clinical Safety Officer, module content owners, implementation lead, interface owners and independent reviewers.

## Hazard log

For each hazard document:

- workflow and triggering condition
- potential harm and affected population
- initial severity and likelihood
- preventive and detective controls
- residual risk
- testing evidence
- owner and review date

## Required high-risk evidence

- patient identity matching and merge testing
- medication allergy/interactions and eMAR safeguards
- critical-result routing and acknowledgement
- referral closure and follow-up escalation
- ADT/bed and wrong-patient/location risks
- downtime and reconciliation
- data migration accuracy
- clinical decision-support validation and override monitoring
- device/analyzer interface validation

## Acceptance

The safety case must state known residual risks, approved mitigations, monitoring measures, rollback triggers and formal signatories.
