# SOC 2 Readiness

SOC 2 is an independent examination of controls at a service organization. This release supplies implementation artefacts and evidence sources; it is not a SOC 2 report.

## Trust Services Categories supported

- **Security:** authentication, access matrices, network segregation, secure headers, audit, incident procedures and secure development controls.
- **Availability:** health checks, resilient database profile, backup/restore procedures, recovery planning and downtime documentation.
- **Processing integrity:** validation, database constraints, state-transition rules, audit histories and automated tests.
- **Confidentiality:** patient-context boundaries, controlled exports, private database network, TLS and access review.
- **Privacy:** consent/proxy fields, retention controls, DPIA template, access accounting and disclosure governance.

## Evidence collection still required

- Approved policies and control ownership.
- Tickets, reviews and approvals over the audit period.
- Access reviews, vulnerability scans and remediation evidence.
- Backup and restore results.
- Incident records and post-incident reviews.
- Vendor due diligence and contracts.
- Availability and performance records.
- Independent CPA examination.
